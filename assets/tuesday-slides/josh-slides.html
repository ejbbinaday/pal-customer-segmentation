Where the approach stands

We stopped asking the machine to invent the segments —
and started asking it to check ours.

✕ What we planned
ML discovers the segments; we name them.
Point a clustering algorithm at 22.9M bookings, let natural groups fall out, attach business names. Standard playbook.

It failed — and we can prove it failed for a real reason. Ten methods across six families, best separation 0.381 where ~0.5 is the usual minimum to claim structure exists.
➜
The pivot
✓ What we do now
Business rules draw the lines; ML checks them.
PAL's commercial logic assigns the segment. The machine's three jobs: split oversized segments, test whether the boundaries hold, and monitor for drift.

More defensible, not less — every boundary is written down and reviewable.

The analogy for the room: a fruit stand has obvious piles — you can spot the gap between apples and oranges across the room. A paint store runs continuously from white to cream to beige, with no natural gap — yet it still needs named colours on the shelf, because nobody orders "wavelength 578nm". PAL's passengers are a paint store. So we choose the boundaries deliberately, for commercial usefulness, instead of pretending we discovered piles.

Agenda 2 · ML success metrics + sample calcJOSH
What we optimise, and why not accuracy

Two models. Identical 90% accuracy. 2.4× different business impact.

A warehouse sorts 1,000 parcels and gets 90% right. Good? It depends entirely on which 10% — swapping two boxes of identical envelopes costs nothing; putting the medical-supplies crate on the wrong truck is serious. Same error rate, different consequence. Segmentation is the same, so we weight errors by what they cost.

Metric 1 · per-segment recall
54.5% vs 95.5%
Of genuine Corporate travellers, the share we caught (24/44 vs 42/44). Target ≥91% — A fails, B passes.
Metric 2 · weighted cost / booking
₱1,120 vs ₱472
Peso-weighted error rate. Minimise. This is the optimisation target, not accuracy.
Why asymmetric
Over-serving
is cheap
Corporate→Budget costs ~₱40,000 (under-served, promo fare quoted). The reverse costs a courtesy upgrade. Weights: Corporate ×10 → Budget ×1.
⚠️ The honest caveat — this is why the SME ask matters. The machinery is built and tested, but today "correct" means "matches what our own rules said" — we are grading our own homework. We have shown the segments carry real signal (they predict refunds and 180-day rebooking, which no rule looks at), so the labels are not noise. What's missing is an answer key. → next slide.
Weights & peso figures are PAL's own estimates (requirements FR-28 / NFR-01), not our invention — they should be confirmed

Agenda 3 · Asks from SMEs — constraintsJOSH
What we need, in what format, in what file

Two constraint files — already created and pre-filled with our guesses.

A blank template gets a blank response, so both files ship with 7 example rules each. The ask is to correct, delete and extend them — a rule we invented and an SME confirmed is worth far more than a rule we invented alone.
Hard
Rules that must never be broken
Statements of impossibility. "Booked 60+ days out, economy, no loyalty ID — cannot be Corporate." These shrink the decision space before anyone makes a judgement call: instead of choosing among 10 segments, an annotator picks among 2–3.
📄 data/constraints/hard_constraints.csv
rule_id,condition,verdict,segments,owner,confidence,notes
H01,lead_days >= 60 AND max_tier <= 4,cannot_be,Corporate,RM Dom,certain,…
H02,channel = 'Sea Crew',must_be,OFW/Migrant,RM Intl,certain,…
H03,is_award,must_be,Mabuhay Loyalist,FF Owner,certain,…
H04,any_business AND lead_days <= 1,narrow_to,Corporate|Prem Bleisure,…
H05,pilgrimage_dest AND max_tier <= 2,cannot_be,Premium Bleisure,…
H06,is_domestic AND NOT any_premium,cannot_be,Premium Bleisure,RM Dom,certain,…
H07,foreign_issue AND max_tier <= 2,cannot_be,Corporate,RM Intl,likely,…
verdict = must_be · cannot_be · narrow_to (shortlist, |-separated).  confidence = certain or likely — only certain is auto-enforced; likely gets tested against the data and brought back to you if it conflicts.
Soft
Tendencies, not laws
A lean. "Middle East bookings tend to be OFW rather than leisure — but a Manila–Dubai holiday is perfectly possible." These tilt ambiguous cases, and they tell us which boundaries PAL considers soft — where we should report ambiguity instead of forcing a confident label.
📄 data/constraints/soft_constraints.csv
rule_id,condition,leans_toward,leans_away_from,strength,owner,notes
S01,dest_region = 'Middle East',OFW/Migrant,Prem Bleisure,strong,…
S02,dep_month IN (4,5,12),Balikbayan/VFR,Corporate,moderate,…
S03,lead_days <= 3 AND max_tier >= 6,Corporate,Last-Minute,strong,…
S04,is_domestic AND n_bookings >= 6,Corporate,Budget/Adv,weak,…
S05,connecting AND NOT round_trip,OFW/Migrant,Family,moderate,RM Intl,…
S06,issue_country='PH' AND intl,Budget/Adv,OFW/Migrant,moderate,…
S07,is_group AND dest='Middle East',Pilgrimage,Family,moderate,…
strength = weak · moderate · strong.  Where a soft constraint contradicts the data, that disagreement is the finding — we bring it back rather than quietly overriding either side. S06 is a candidate rule for the 9.58% Unassigned gap.
Ask 3 — the answer key · data/labels/sme_sample.csv
~1,000 bookings labelled by hand. We supply route, dates, lead time, cabin, fare tier, channel, group flag, issue country, revenue band — nothing more. SME fills true_segment, confidence, notes. "Unsure" is a first-class answer — forcing a guess manufactures noise we cannot detect. Stratified, not random (a random 1,000 yields ~2 Pilgrimage, 0 Mabuhay). ~100 rows go to every SME: where SMEs disagree with each other, that rate is a hard ceiling on any accuracy we could ever claim. 8–16 hours total across 2–3 SMEs. Scorer is already written.
Decisions only PAL can make
• 6 segments or 10? Requirements say 6; the model produces 10.
• How is PH-issued outbound international economy segmented? The 9.58% gap.
• OFW/Migrant vs Balikbayan/VFR — one segment or two? 6.8M bookings on one bit.
• Are the penalty weights right? ×10 and ₱40,000 drive the whole optimisation.
• Can we get Mabuhay tier? Without it that segment stays invisible.
CSV is deliberate — opens in Excel, condition accepts near-plain language. Prose in an email is fine; we transcribe. Format must never be the blocker
